# Calculator — User Guide

## Opening the app

Go to: **https://hermesagenttabbeqai.github.io/calculator/**

No installation needed. Works in Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge.

---

## Basic calculation

1. Click the digit buttons (or use your keyboard) to enter a number.
2. Click an operator: **+**, **−**, **×**, or **÷**.
3. Enter a second number.
4. Press **=** (or **Enter**) to see the result.

**Example:** `7 × 8 = 56`

---

## Clearing and correcting

| Button | Keyboard | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **C** | Escape | Clears everything, resets to 0 |
| **⌫** | Backspace | Deletes the last character |

---

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key(s) | Action |
|---|---|
| `0` – `9`, `.` | Enter digits / decimal point |
| `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` | Enter an operator |
| `Enter` or `=` | Calculate |
| `Backspace` | Delete last character |
| `Escape` | Clear |

---

## Copying the result

Click the **Copy** button next to the result to copy it to your clipboard. You can then paste it into any other app.

---

## Calculation history

The **History** panel below the calculator shows your last 5 calculations. It is saved in your browser and survives page reloads.

- **Click any entry** to load its result back into the calculator.
- The history is specific to your browser and device.

---

## Error messages

| Message | Cause |
|---|---|
| `Invalid input` | Expression is empty or malformed (e.g. `5 + + 3`) |
| `Cannot divide by 0` | You tried to divide a number by zero |

Press **C** to clear the error and start a new calculation.
